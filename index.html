Rules

How to Play: Guided Tutorial

Enter the Interpretation

The owner of this Interpretation should state the historical relationship between the two Evidence pieces. Enter only the interpretation itself; discussion about whether it is convincing can happen verbally.

Accepted Interpretations count toward the argument network. Rejected Interpretations remain visible on the board but do not score. No written justification for the class decision is required.

Challenge an Interpretation

A challenge must cite one of your own Evidence cards. Spend one of your two Challenge tokens to question the historical validity of this interpretation.

Historical Blocker

During your Evidence step, you may try to break through this blocker with your currently selected Evidence card. If the class accepts the refutation, the block is replaced by that Evidence piece and counts as one of your two Evidence placements.
